# Scarborough map and street expansion

Eight real destinations are baked from OpenStreetMap in `data/osm/scarborough.json`, with OSM feature IDs, coordinates, tags and snapshot provenance. Run `node scripts/bake-scarborough.mjs` to refresh those anchors. The existing ODbL licence and in-game OSM credit apply. The destination selection was cross-checked against the City of Toronto's [Our Scarborough Centre destination map](https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/9654-city-planning-our-scarborough-phase4-final-study-report.pdf).

The playable map remains compressed. Mall/campus exteriors, surrounding street blocks and game cells are artistic interpretations, not traced architectural footprints. Existing station positions are retained to protect transit routes and saves. Scarborough now extends five more blocks east, with shopping hubs at Agincourt, Bridlewood, Woodside, Malvern and Cedarbrae; study destinations at Centennial Progress, Centennial Morningside and UTSC. Generated campus, employment, apartment and residential blocks surround them.

`blockForms` is the common contract for building massing and the entrance catalogue. Bungalows, pitched-roof houses, semis, modern houses, terraces, slab apartments, towers, retail strips, warehouses and campuses have different dimensions and silhouettes. Low-detail scenery reuses the same forms. Ground navigation still uses sidewalks, crossings and explicit entrance paths. Save versions reset obsolete street/interior positions without resetting money or progress.

The city camera allows a small left/right turn, not a full orbit. Detailed rendering stays at 25 cells; an instanced 144-cell outer ring continues outside the playable boundary. A continuous water plane covers the horizon at the waterfront. Zoom limits are shared with geometric frustum tests at 1440, 1024 and 390 pixels, both zoom limits, both turn limits and playable-world corners. Visual browser review remains necessary; these tests validate coverage and collision geometry, not rendered appearance.
